# Viral Hook Patterns & Retention Playbook

Short-form video platforms (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels) judge a video's quality in the first 1.5 to 3.0 seconds. If a viewer scrolls, retention score plunges.

## 1. Golden Rules of High-Retention Hooks

1. **Ban the Clichés**:
   - ❌ Never start with: _"Did you know..."_, _"Have you ever wondered..."_, _"In today's video..."_, _"Hey guys..."_, or _"Let me tell you about..."_ (unless explicitly requested by niche rules).
   - ✅ Instead, drop the viewer straight into high-stakes action, immediate danger, or an unresolved paradox.

2. **The 3-Second Curiosity Loop**:
   - Establish a question in the viewer's subconscious mind that _cannot_ be answered without watching until the end.
   - Reveal an unbelievable result first, but hide the bizarre mechanism.

3. **Pattern Interrupts**:
   - Use jarring contrasting imagery, counter-intuitive claims, or shocking historical realities.
   - Example: _"Historians were horrified when they opened this tomb..."_
   - Example: _"This pilot turned off both engines at 30,000 feet on purpose..."_

---

## 2. Proven Hook Frameworks

### Framework A: The High-Stakes Disaster / Cover-Up

> Formula: `[Authority / System] + [Desperate or Horrific Action] + [Unforeseen Consequence]`

- _"The Soviet Union spent 12 years digging the deepest hole on Earth, then sealed it with concrete and never went back."_
- _"NASA cut the live feed for 30 seconds when Apollo 11 entered the lunar shadow."_

### Framework B: The Absurd Paradox

> Formula: `[Extreme Effort / Success] + [Completely Contradictory Outcome]`

- _"A man literally saved 80,000 lives from nuclear war, and was fired the next morning."_
- _"He stole $200 million without leaving a single fingerprint, but got caught buying a $3 hot dog."_

### Framework C: The Stoic Cold Open

> Formula: `[Brutal Reality of Human Nature] + [Ancient Strategic Advantage]`

- _"Marcus Aurelius ruled the known world, but wrote in his private diary that everyone he met was ungrateful."_
- _"You aren't tired from working too hard. You're exhausted from caring about things you can't control."_

### Framework D: The Crime Escalation

> Formula: `[Infiltration / Deception] + [Ticking Clock / Fatal Flaw]`

- _"An engineer spent 18 months secretly sabotaging Ferrari from inside their own factory."_
- _"He walked into the world's most secure diamond vault in Antwerp and walked out with $100 million in his gym bag."_

---

## 3. Retaining Viewers Through Scene Transitions

- **Micro-Cliffhangers (Every 10-15s)**:
  - At second 10: _"But that wasn't the worst part."_
  - At second 25: _"When investigators examined the wreckage, they found something impossible."_
- **The Final Loop**:
  - Write the final sentence so that it flows seamlessly back into the opening hook when the video repeats automatically.
