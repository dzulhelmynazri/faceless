# Short-Form Video Pacing & Calibration Playbook

Short-form algorithmic retention relies heavily on vocal rhythm, word pacing, and rapid visual scene changes.

## 1. Natural Spoken Cadence

The human brain processes vertical video voiceover best at:

- **Pacing Standard**: **2.3 to 2.5 words per second** (approx. 140 – 150 words per minute).
- Slower than 2.1 words/sec feels sluggish and causes swiping.
- Faster than 2.7 words/sec sacrifices emotional resonance and clarity.

## 2. Word Count Budgets by Target Duration

| Target Duration | Optimal Word Count | Scene Count | Average Scene Duration |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **30-40 seconds** | 75 – 95 words | 3 – 5 scenes | 5 – 7 seconds |
| **60-70 seconds** | 140 – 170 words | 5 – 8 scenes | 6 – 8 seconds |

## 3. Scene Breakdown & Visual Prompts

Every script must be segmented into consecutive visual scenes:

1. **Scene Duration**: Never keep the camera on a single static image for longer than 6 seconds to prevent viewer fatigue.
2. **Visual Continuity**: Maintain characters, color grading, and era throughout all scenes.
3. **9:16 Vertical Composition**:
   - Explicitly instruct camera position (e.g. _low-angle dramatic shot_, _cinematic aerial drone tracking_, _macro close-up on sweating hands_).
   - Detail atmosphere, volumetric fog, lighting (e.g., _chiaroscuro_, _neon cyberpunk rim lighting_, _vintage 35mm grain_).
   - Match the series `artStyleId` precisely.
